# Pravaha — Market Intelligence Report

**Date:** 2026-03-30
**Analyst:** Vartika (market) + Anveshon (research) + Sangraha (data)

---

## Executive Summary

The market for free Twitter/X viewing alternatives is large (Nitter had 10M+ monthly users at peak) but underserved. Nitter's decline has created a vacuum. No credible replacement exists that offers reliable timelines + search without payment or X account.

## TAM/SAM/SOM

| Metric | Estimate | Basis |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **TAM** | 50M users/year | Privacy-conscious internet users who want Twitter content without an account |
| **SAM** | 15M users/year | Active Nitter/XCancel users + researchers + journalists in English-speaking markets |
| **SOM** | 500K users/year (Y1) | Realistic capture with organic growth + tech community adoption |

## Revenue Model (Future)

- **Free tier:** Core functionality (timelines, search, single feeds)
- **Premium tier ($5/month):** Feed builder, alerts, export, compare view, priority data sources
- **API tier ($20/month):** Programmatic access for researchers/developers

## Competitive Landscape Detail

### Nitter/XCancel
- **Status:** Degrading. Requires real account session tokens since 2025. Domain expires May 2026.
- **UX:** Functional but dated. Looks like 2015 Twitter.
- **Users:** Declining from peak. Intermittent outages push users away.
- **Threat:** Low — dying platform, not improving.

### Thread Reader App
- **Status:** Active, sustainable (ad-supported)
- **UX:** Good for threads, but single-purpose
- **Users:** ~5M monthly
- **Threat:** Low — different use case (threads only)

### FixTweet/FxTwitter
- **Status:** Active, well-maintained OSS
- **UX:** Not a browsing product — it's an embed fixer
- **Users:** Millions (via Discord/Telegram embeds)
- **Threat:** None — complementary technology, not competing product

### Commercial Providers (TwitterAPI.io, SociaVault)
- **Status:** Active, growing
- **UX:** API-only, not consumer products
- **Users:** Developers, enterprises
- **Threat:** None — different market (B2B API vs B2C product)

## Key Insight

**The gap is a RELIABLE, PREMIUM, FREE consumer product for browsing public tweets.** Nobody occupies this space. Nitter is dying. Thread Reader is threads-only. FixTweet is embeds-only. Commercial providers are B2B APIs.

Pravaha fills this exact gap.
